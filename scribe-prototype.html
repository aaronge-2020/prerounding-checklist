About this prototype

EngineTransformers.js (vendored) + ONNX Runtime WASM
Devicewasm · 1 thread (phone path, no WebGPU)
Segmentationenergy VAD → utterance chunks
Speaker memoryCAM++ embedding (28 MB) · enroll once, cosine match per utterance
Networkmodel download only · inference fully local

Patterns borrowed from meetscribe's on-device work (MIT): worker-isolated inference with downloadable model files streamed into the browser cache, capability-based plan choice, energy-VAD audio chunking. Parakeet TDT was evaluated and rejected for phone use — meetscribe's own capability check warns iOS Safari will crash on its ~1.5 GB RAM need.

Not validated for clinical documentation. Transcription accuracy has not been evaluated on real clinical speech, drug names are a known weak spot in early testing, and every transcript requires clinician review before it informs care or enters any record. Nothing here is medical advice.
